1. Introduction

Welcome to PuppyGrid! This Privacy Policy explains what information PuppyGrid handles, why we use it, who may receive it, how long it may be kept, and what choices you have.

We collect only the information reasonably needed to operate, support, advertise, and improve the game.

2. Information We Handle

PuppyGrid saves gameplay information on your device, including completed and unlocked levels, puzzle progress, achievements, daily puzzle streaks, hint balances, settings, preferences, and unlocked cosmetic items.

The game and its service providers may also process information for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, purchases, and basic operation. This may include device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location based on IP address, device model, operating system, app version, language, gameplay events, ad interactions, purchase details, and crash data.

Gameplay saved locally is not automatically sent to Maclow Games. Integrated third-party services may transmit information to their providers.

3. Personal Information

You do not need an account or have to provide your name, email address, or phone number to play PuppyGrid. Service providers may process technical, advertising, usage and transaction information that may be personal data under applicable law. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the game.

If you contact us, we may receive your email address and message to respond and maintain support records.

4. In-App Purchases

PuppyGrid may offer optional purchases, including hints and cosmetic items. Google Play Billing processes payments; PuppyGrid does not request payment-card details directly. Google Play and providers may use purchase data to authorize, verify, deliver, prevent fraud, support and restore purchases. We may receive purchase status, product and transaction identifiers, and purchase dates, but not full payment-card numbers.

5. Advertising, Analytics and Crash Reporting

The planned Android service integrations described by this policy include Google AdMob (advertising), Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting), and Google Play Billing (purchases). These integrations must be checked against the actual published app build.

  • Advertising: Google partner sites privacy information; Ads Settings.
  • Analytics: Google privacy information.
  • Crash reporting: Firebase Privacy and Security.
  • Payments: Google Payments Privacy Notice and Google Play Terms.

These providers may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, technical details, app usage, ad interactions and diagnostic information. Where required, we will request consent for personalized advertising or other processing that requires consent. Available choices may include in-game consent tools, device privacy settings and provider controls.

6. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers needed for game distribution, payments, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, hosting, security and support. Providers may process information in other countries. We may disclose information to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud or enforce agreements.

We do not sell player information for money. Certain advertising-related processing may be considered a sale or sharing under some laws; where required, applicable choices or opt-out methods will be provided. Local gameplay data is not automatically shared with other players.

7. Retention, Deletion and Your Rights

Local gameplay data remains until you delete it, clear app data, uninstall PuppyGrid, or your device removes it. Clearing data may permanently remove progress and unlocks. Third-party providers retain information under their own policies. We keep information as reasonably necessary for support, security, fraud prevention, transactions, disputes and legal obligations.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, request a copy, opt out of targeted advertising or complain to a privacy authority.

For privacy requests, email mclwgames55@gmail.com with subject “Privacy Request.” Identify PuppyGrid, describe the request and provide your location if relevant. Do not send passwords, card numbers or unnecessary sensitive information. We may request reasonable identity or authority verification. We generally respond to verified requests within 30 days, with extensions where permitted by law. We may decline requests when verification is insufficient or a legal exception applies, explaining the decision and applicable appeal rights where required.

Some information is held by independent providers and may require contacting them directly. To limit personalized advertising, use applicable in-game tools, device advertising settings and provider controls. You may still see less-personalized advertising.

8. Children's Privacy

PuppyGrid is family-friendly but is not intended to knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. The game does not require personal information to play. Audience settings, advertising, consent and data practices will follow applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play Families policies. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable law.

9. Data Security

We aim to minimize information needed to operate PuppyGrid and use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards with external providers. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when features, services or data practices change. Updates will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date and additional notice or consent where required.

11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This policy is governed by laws applicable to the developer and processing activities. Mandatory privacy and consumer rights in your location remain applicable. Disputes will be handled by courts with jurisdiction under applicable law.
